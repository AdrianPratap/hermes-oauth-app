Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Hermes Automation is a personal-use application built and operated solely by Adrian Pratap, for his own use only. It is not distributed to, marketed to, or used by any other individual or organisation.

This application accesses Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts) belonging exclusively to the owner's own Google account, using OAuth 2.0 authorisation granted directly by the owner to himself.

Data retrieved through these APIs is used only to perform actions the owner directly requests (e.g. reading email, managing calendar events, editing documents) and is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Data is stored only locally, under the owner's own control, and never transmitted to any third-party service beyond the Google APIs themselves.

Access can be revoked at any time by the owner via Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

Questions: contact adrian.pratap@gmail.com.
